Privacy Policy

Somi's Orchard: Bubble Match3

Effective date: November 25, 2026

ChachaDaun ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the mobile game "Somi's Orchard: Bubble Match3" (the "Game") handles and how.

1. Information we collect directly

We do not collect information that identifies you, such as your name, email address, or phone number. The Game has no account sign-up, and we do not run a server that receives your information.

2. Information stored only on your device

To save your progress, the Game stores the following only on your device. It is never sent to us.

  • Best score, collection records, stage stars, tutorial progress
  • Sound, music, vibration, and screen shake settings
  • Number of games played (used to space out full-screen ads)

You can erase your game records with Reset Data in Settings. Uninstalling the Game removes everything.

3. Information handled by third-party services

a. Google AdMob (advertising)

InformationAdvertising ID, IP address, device information (model, OS, language, etc.), ad impressions and clicks, approximate location inferred from IP address
PurposeShowing ads, measuring ad performance, preventing fraud, and personalized ads where you have consented
ProcessorGoogle LLC

See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

b. Google Play Games Services (leaderboards)

InformationPlay Games profile ID, profile name, profile picture, submitted scores
PurposeRecording and showing leaderboards
ProcessorGoogle LLC

Submitted scores and your Play Games profile name and picture are visible to other players. You can change your profile visibility in the Play Games app. If you do not sign in, nothing is submitted.

c. Unity (game engine)

The Unity engine may send anonymous device statistics (device model, OS, graphics capabilities) to Unity Technologies. This information cannot identify you.

4. International transfers

The information in Section 3 is transferred to and processed in the United States and other countries where Google and Unity operate, under their respective policies.

5. Retention

  • On-device information is kept until you reset your data or uninstall the Game.
  • Information handled by third-party services is retained under their policies. We do not keep copies.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Ad privacy settings: Where required by law (such as the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), the Game asks for your consent on first launch. You can change your choice any time with Ad Privacy Settings in the Game's Settings.
  • Personalized ads and Advertising ID: On Android, go to Settings > Privacy (or Google) > Ads to delete or reset your Advertising ID.
  • Leaderboards: Change your profile visibility in the Play Games app, or do not sign in.
  • Game records: Use Reset Data in Settings, or uninstall the Game.

EEA/UK users: We rely on your consent for personalized advertising and on legitimate interests for non-personalized ads and fraud prevention. You have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict, or object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California and other U.S. state residents: We do not sell your personal information for money. Advertising through Google AdMob may be considered "sharing" for targeted advertising under some state laws; you can opt out by deleting or resetting your Advertising ID as described above.

To make any request, contact us below. We hold no personal data ourselves, but we will help you reach the relevant service.

7. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (under 14 in Korea, or the applicable age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Ads in the Game are limited to content rated PG (parental guidance) or lower.

8. Security

We do not store your information on our servers. On-device records contain no information that identifies you. Information sent to third-party services uses their encrypted connections.
